# Pixel Office (Orca plugin)

A big pixel-art office in an editor tab: the CEO's office with a reception, C-level offices, an open space, a lounge.
Every Orca bot has an office and walks where its real status puts it; idle ones wander and chat in icon clouds
(«Жизнь», animation only, no tokens). The building is `office/map.json`; looks and themes are skin packs in
`office/skins/`; who sits where, pins and decor choices live in the plugin storage. MIT licensed.

See the [project README](../README.md) and [how to make a skin pack](../docs/skin-packs.md).
